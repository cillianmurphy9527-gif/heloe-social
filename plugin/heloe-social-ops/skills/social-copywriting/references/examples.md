## LinkedIn · teardown (signals)
Hook: a concrete trigger event.
> A company posts 3 SDR roles in one week.
>
> Most sellers see a job post.
> We see a team about to inherit a pipeline problem.
>
> What I'd send the founder (within 72 hours):
> "Saw you're hiring 3 SDRs. Usually that means the pipeline target just went up before the team can ramp. ..."
>
> Why it works:
> → It's about their week, not your product.
> → It names the likely pain, not a feature.
> → It's sent while the decision is still open.
>
> Same message 3 months later? Just noise.
>
